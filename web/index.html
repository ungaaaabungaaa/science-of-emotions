READ THE FINE PRINT

Evidence has
boundaries.

01 / WHAT WE COUNT

Published annotations.

Each comment contributes once to each of its released labels. Pair counts are intersections of those labels. The source contains English Reddit comments selected and labeled by the original research team.

02 / WHAT WE LEARN

Overlap and imbalance.

Some labels occur much more often than others. Multiple labels can coexist, including neutral with an emotion label. A category is not a discrete biological measurement.

03 / WHAT WE CANNOT CLAIM

No mind reading.

These rates do not represent all people, cultures, or languages. Annotation choices, platform demographics, and content filtering affect them. We do not infer mental state, diagnose, or reproduce the paper's BERT results.

04 / REPEAT THE WORK

Four files. Locked hashes.

Source files are pinned to a Git revision and SHA-256 checked. The pipeline rejects malformed records and repeated comment IDs. The viewer uses aggregate counts only; it does not collect personal text.
