# Third-party sources

The report and its figures derive from **GoEmotions**, developed at Google Research. Its model card explicitly identifies the data license as Apache 2.0; a copy is retained in [docs/GOEMOTIONS-LICENSE.txt](docs/GOEMOTIONS-LICENSE.txt). Raw comments are fetched only on request and excluded from the repository and viewer. No upstream analysis/model implementation was copied.

Citation: Dorottya Demszky, Dana Movshovitz-Attias, Jeongwoo Ko, Alan Cowen, Gaurav Nemade, and Sujith Ravi. 2020. *GoEmotions: A Dataset of Fine-Grained Emotions*. ACL, pages 4040–4054. [DOI 10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.372](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.372).

The [pinned source](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/700de9630a39cf1c0ded1ea7d1092ec0f467c7a7/goemotions) and [model card](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/blob/700de9630a39cf1c0ded1ea7d1092ec0f467c7a7/goemotions/goemotions_model_card.pdf) describe collection, licensing, and limitations. Selection/annotation bias and potentially problematic language remain. This adaptation publishes only computed aggregates, without source text or identities.

The research guide links additional papers without reproducing their text or figures. Screenshots show this running application. The prevalence figure comes from the real report. No generated concept cover is included pending user approval.
